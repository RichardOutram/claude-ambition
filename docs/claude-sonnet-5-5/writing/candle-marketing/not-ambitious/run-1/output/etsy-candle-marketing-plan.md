**Ongoing:** check Etsy Stats monthly. Refresh the titles and tags of weak listings. Renew or re-list seasonally.

## 5. Product & Pricing Strategy

- **Core line:** 3–5 signature scents, with two sizes each (e.g., 8 oz and 12 oz).
- **Entry item:** a mini or sample set ($8–12) to lower the risk of a first purchase.
- **Bundles:** trio sets, "build your own" boxes, and gift sets. These raise average order value.
- **Seasonal drops:** limited editions create urgency (see calendar below).
- **Pricing:** cost of materials + labor + Etsy fees (~6.5% transaction + payment processing + listing fee) + shipping goal + 30–50% margin. Don't underprice; candles at $5 signal low quality.
- **Free shipping threshold:** set slightly above your average order value (e.g., free over $35) to encourage add-ons.

## 6. Etsy Tools

- **Etsy Ads:** start at $1–3/day on your 2–3 best-converting listings. Review after 30 days; keep only listings with a positive return.
- **Sales & discounts:** use for holidays, "first order" coupons, and abandoned-cart offers. Offers to people who favorited an item often convert well.
- **Etsy's "Star Seller" targets:** respond to messages within 24 hours, ship on time, and keep reviews high. Star Seller status improves visibility.
- **Reviews:** include a thank-you card asking for an honest review. Reply to every review.

## 8. Email & Customer Retention

Etsy doesn't hand you customer emails to market to, so build your own list.

- Put a **QR code or card** in each package: "Get 10% off your next order + early access to new scents."
- Use a free tier of Mailchimp, MailerLite, or Flodesk. Comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR (only add people who opt in).
- **Cadence:** 2 emails/month — new releases, restock alerts, seasonal gift guides.
- **Etsy-side retention:** offer a coupon to past buyers through Etsy's built-in marketing tools; follow up with a thank-you message after delivery.

## 9. Packaging & Unboxing

Packaging is marketing.
- Branded tissue, sticker seals, a handwritten note, a care card.
- A free sample (mini candle or wax melt) for repeat or large orders.
- Make it photo-worthy to encourage customers to share it.
- Include social handles and the email-signup QR code.

# Part 2: Going Deeper

## 16. Unit Economics (Know Your Numbers First)

Work this out per product before spending on ads. Example for a 8 oz candle priced at $28:

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Wax, fragrance, wick, jar, label | $6.50 |
| Packaging (box, tissue, insert) | $1.80 |
| Labor (20 min at $18/hr) | $6.00 |
| Etsy fees (~6.5% + ~3% + $0.25 processing + $0.20 listing) | ~$2.20 |
| Offsite Ads fee, if it applies (12–15%) | $0–4.20 |
| **Total cost** | **~$16.50–20.70** |
| **Profit** | **~$7.30–11.50 (26–41%)** |

**Why it matters:**
- **Break-even ROAS** = price ÷ profit. At $9 profit on $28, break-even ROAS is about 3.1×. Ads below that lose money on first purchase.
- **Max customer acquisition cost** = profit on first order + expected profit from repeats. If 20% reorder twice, you can afford a bit more than $9 per customer.
- **Shipping:** don't absorb it blindly. Build it into price, then offer "free shipping" as a framing.
- **Offsite Ads:** if you earn over $10k/yr you can't opt out and the fee drops to 12%. Under that, consider opting out if margins are thin, or price in the fee.

Create a simple spreadsheet with one row per SKU: cost, price, margin, break-even ROAS, units sold.

## 17. Keyword Research Process

1. **Seed list:** type each product idea into Etsy's search bar and note every autocomplete suggestion.
2. **Competitor scan:** open the top 10 results for your 5 best terms. Note their titles, tags, price, photo style, review count.
3. **Tools:** eRank, Marmalead, or Alura (free tiers work to start) for search volume vs. competition.
4. **Score each phrase:** high demand, low competition, and relevant to *your* product beats a broad term like "candle."
5. **Group by intent:**
   - *Product:* soy candle, amber jar candle, wooden wick candle
   - *Scent:* lavender vanilla candle, cedar and smoke, fig candle
   - *Occasion/gift:* housewarming gift, teacher gift, bridesmaid gift, sympathy gift
   - *Style/aesthetic:* cottagecore, minimalist, boho, dark academia
   - *Benefit:* relaxing, self care, aromatherapy, spa gift
6. **Assign** one primary phrase per listing. Avoid making your own listings compete with each other.

**Example title formula:**
`[Primary phrase] | [Scent] | [Material/size] | [Gift angle]`
→ `Lavender Vanilla Soy Candle | Hand Poured 8oz Wooden Wick | Relaxing Self Care Gift for Her`

## 19. Etsy Ads Playbook

- **Phase 1 (weeks 1–4):** $2/day on 2 listings with the best conversion rate and good reviews. Don't judge before about 100 clicks.
- **Phase 2:** if ROAS is above break-even (see §16), raise budget by about 20% every week, and add 1 new listing at a time.
- **Phase 3:** pause listings with 50+ clicks and no sales; fix the listing (photos, price, title) before re-enabling.
- **Seasonal:** raise budget 3–4 weeks before peak events (Mother's Day, Black Friday, Christmas) and lower it afterward.
- **Check:** only count sales attributed to ads, and compare against your organic baseline.

## 24. Subscription & Repeat-Revenue Ideas

- **Candle of the Month** (via Etsy variations or an off-Etsy store): lower the price slightly for commitment.
- **Refill program:** return jars for a discount (sustainability angle).
- **Loyalty card:** buy 5, get a mini free (include in packaging).
- **Scent quiz** on your site/social to guide purchases and capture emails.

## 25. Reputation & Reviews System

- Insert a card: "Loved it? A review helps a small shop more than you know."
- Respond to *all* reviews in 24–48 hours; never argue publicly.
- For issues: reply fast, offer a replacement or refund, and keep the tone warm. A solved problem often becomes a good review.
- Feature the best quotes in listing photos and social posts.

## 28. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Overdependence on Etsy | Build email list, own site (Shopify/Squarespace) by month 6 |
| Seasonal cash crunch | Pre-buy materials with Q3 profits; offer pre-orders |
| Shipping damage / melting | Insulated packaging in summer; pick shipping days; photograph packed items |
| Safety/legal issues | Product liability insurance, proper labeling, batch records |
| Copycats | Distinct brand story, unique scents, trademark your name if growing |
| Burnout | Batch production, cap weekly orders, set realistic handling times |
| Negative review | Respond quickly, resolve, learn |

## 29. Scaling Path

1. **Months 0–3:** optimize listings, build review base, test ads.
2. **Months 3–6:** launch email list and seasonal drops; try markets; reach consistent monthly sales.
3. **Months 6–12:** wholesale to 3–5 boutiques; launch own website; hire part-time help for pouring/packing.
4. **Year 2:** expand into related products (wax melts, room sprays, diffusers, gift boxes); subscription; bulk/corporate channel.
