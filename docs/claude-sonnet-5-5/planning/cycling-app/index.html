What we look for

Rubric: whether it finds a real reason for cyclists to switch (a wedge Strava and Zwift don't cover) rather than generic badges and leaderboards; whether the Expo specifics hold up (background GPS and battery, BLE sensors such as power meters and heart-rate straps, HealthKit and Health Connect, Strava import, what needs a dev build rather than Expo Go, EAS); app store risks (background location review, privacy zones around home); an MVP small enough for one or two people with measurable milestones; how the gamification avoids rewarding unsafe riding; and how the plan would be checked against real riders before building it all.
