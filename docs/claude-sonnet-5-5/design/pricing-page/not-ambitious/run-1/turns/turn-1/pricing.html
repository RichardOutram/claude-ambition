Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

Not for Starter. Paid plans include a 14-day free trial, and you'll only be asked for a card if you decide to keep going.

What happens if I go over my Starter limits?

We'll never lock you out of your data. You can keep viewing and exporting everything; to send more invoices you'll just need to upgrade or wait for next month.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades or cancellations take effect at the end of your billing period. No contracts, no cancellation fees.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

No. The payment processor charges its standard fee (2.9% + 30¢ for cards); Quillbill adds nothing on top.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?

Yes — 30% off any paid plan. Email us from your school or organization address and we'll set it up.

What if I'm not happy?

Within 30 days of your first payment, ask and we'll refund it in full.
