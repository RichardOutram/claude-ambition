Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Starter is free forever with no card, and the 14-day trials of Freelancer and Studio don't ask for one either.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel in two clicks from your settings. Changes are prorated, and if you cancel you keep access until the period ends.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

We never add fees on top of your payment processor. You pay only the standard processor rate (for example, Stripe's), and nothing extra to us.

What happens if I go over the Starter limits?

Nothing breaks. Existing invoices stay accessible, and we'll offer an upgrade when you want to add more clients or invoices.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?

Yes, 50% off for students and registered nonprofits. Contact support with proof of status and we'll sort it out.

Can I export my data?

Always. Download invoices, clients and payment history as CSV or PDF at any time, on any plan.
