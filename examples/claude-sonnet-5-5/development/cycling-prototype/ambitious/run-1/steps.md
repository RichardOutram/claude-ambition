| Step | What it did | New or changed in the work |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · First version | Plain Claude's prototype, unseen in a browser. | 16 screens covering most of the plan, with SVG squiggles for maps and placeholder toasts on some taps. |
| 2 · Altitude | Four questions. The stand-in wanted a rider or investor to get through the core loop with no help, and named four dead taps to fix. | **Fixed the four dead taps** (notifications, share, new club, settings). **Added** in-context explainers for XP and the streak, a guided first ride, and `rider-test-script.md` (seven tasks and an observation sheet). Clicked all 180 controls. |
| 3 · Depth | axe-core accessibility, a 44 px tap-target audit and a data-consistency check, at a small-phone size. | **Fixed** 17 accessibility violations and 45 small targets, and Finish and Pause hidden behind the tab bar on small phones. **Made the ride believable:** realistic pace, an XP breakdown that adds up, a first-ride badge instead of "Climber" on a flat 0.5 km. |
| 4 · Challenge | Six assumptions tested with a probe script. | **Found six bugs:** Finish at 0:00 invented a 14.8 km ride; completing a challenge never paid its XP; the streak card contradicted itself; two sheets stacked; phone users couldn't reset or leave Pro. All fixed. |
| 5 · Range | Borrowed from product design, game design, research ops and aviation human factors, plus a red team (20 attack checks). | **Added:** a "Next up" goal line, **a facilitator's session log** (timestamped taps, hesitations, time through the core loop), and a confirmation before discarding a ride. **Red team found:** typed text ran as live code (`<img onerror>`), long names broke the layout, turning off the privacy zone silently exposed home, and the paywall had no price after the trial, no cancel info and no "Not now". All fixed. |
| 6 · Judge | Same checks on the first version and the final, plus a cold read of 15 screens. | Plan coverage 44/44 (first version 29 on a weaker check), 0 accessibility violations, 0 small targets. **The cold read found** live XP starting at 20 and a units toggle that did nothing (removed). |
| 7 · Stretch | Core loop in fewer taps, and a 4,000-action random fuzz. | 7 → 5 taps. **The fuzzer found a design bug:** buying a streak freeze spent the XP that sets your level, so buying dropped you a level. Freezes are now earned. |
| 8 · Lock | `checks/run.sh` (all suites), `CLAUDE.md`. | 15 check files left in the project. The visuals are broadly the same as the first version. |

**Value added:** a far sturdier, more honest prototype to test with real riders, plus the session log. But most of the gain is in places you have to click into, the look barely changed, and it took 69 minutes. That's why it's only slightly better than the plain push.
