- **Brand voice:** warm, sensory, personal. Describe how a scent *feels*, not just what's in it.

## 3. Target Customers

1. **Self-gifters / home decor lovers** (25–45): want cozy, aesthetic ambiance.
2. **Gift buyers**: birthdays, housewarming, teachers, weddings, Mother's Day, holidays.
3. **Corporate/event buyers**: bulk favors, client gifts, bridal parties.
4. **Wellness seekers**: aromatherapy, self-care, clean ingredients.

## 4. Etsy SEO (Highest-Leverage Channel)

**Listing titles:** lead with the phrase buyers search, e.g. "Soy Candle, Lavender Vanilla, Hand Poured Gift for Her".

**Tags (all 13 used):** mix long-tail phrases such as `soy wax candle`, `housewarming gift`, `scented candle gift`, `self care gift`, `cozy home decor`, `[scent] candle`.

**Attributes:** fill in all fields (scent, occasion, material, color).

**Descriptions:** put the key details in the first 160 characters, then include scent notes, burn time, wax and wick type, size, and safety info.

**Photos (use all 10 slots):**
1. Hero shot, clean and well lit
2. Lit, in a lifestyle setting
3. Scale shot with hand or object
4. Scent notes graphic
5. Packaging / gift-ready view
6. Close-up of texture and label
7. Collection / bundle shot
8. Burn time and care instructions
9. Customer photo or review
10. Short video (5–15 sec)

**Ongoing:** check Etsy Stats monthly. Refresh the titles and tags of weak listings. Renew or re-list seasonally.

## 5. Product & Pricing Strategy

- **Core line:** 3–5 signature scents, with two sizes each (e.g., 8 oz and 12 oz).
- **Entry item:** a mini or sample set ($8–12) to lower the risk of a first purchase.
- **Bundles:** trio sets, "build your own" boxes, and gift sets. These raise average order value.
- **Seasonal drops:** limited editions create urgency (see calendar below).
- **Pricing:** cost of materials + labor + Etsy fees (~6.5% transaction + payment processing + listing fee) + shipping goal + 30–50% margin. Don't underprice; candles at $5 signal low quality.
- **Free shipping threshold:** set slightly above your average order value (e.g., free over $35) to encourage add-ons.

## 6. Etsy Tools

- **Etsy Ads:** start at $1–3/day on your 2–3 best-converting listings. Review after 30 days; keep only listings with a positive return.
- **Sales & discounts:** use for holidays, "first order" coupons, and abandoned-cart offers. Offers to people who favorited an item often convert well.
- **Etsy's "Star Seller" targets:** respond to messages within 24 hours, ship on time, and keep reviews high. Star Seller status improves visibility.
- **Reviews:** include a thank-you card asking for an honest review. Reply to every review.

## 7. Social Media

Pick **two** platforms and do them well.

| Platform | Content | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (Reels + Stories) | Pouring process, lit-candle ambiance, scent stories, packing orders | 3–4 posts/week |
| **TikTok** | "Day in the life of a candle maker," ASMR pours, small-business packing videos | 3–5 videos/week |
| **Pinterest** | Pins linking to listings (cozy decor, gift guides). Slow burn, but long-lived traffic | 5–10 pins/week |

**Content pillars:**
1. Behind the scenes (making, testing, sourcing)
2. Lifestyle/aesthetic (candles in rooms, seasonal styling)
3. Education (candle care, wick trimming, why soy wax)
4. Social proof (reviews, customer photos)
5. Offers & launches

**Tips:** link to Etsy directly in bio (or use a link hub); use a consistent set of hashtags (`#handmadecandles #soycandles #smallbusiness #etsyshop`); reply to every comment.

## 8. Email & Customer Retention

Etsy doesn't hand you customer emails to market to, so build your own list.

- Put a **QR code or card** in each package: "Get 10% off your next order + early access to new scents."
- Use a free tier of Mailchimp, MailerLite, or Flodesk. Comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR (only add people who opt in).
- **Cadence:** 2 emails/month — new releases, restock alerts, seasonal gift guides.
- **Etsy-side retention:** offer a coupon to past buyers through Etsy's built-in marketing tools; follow up with a thank-you message after delivery.

## 9. Packaging & Unboxing

Packaging is marketing.
- Branded tissue, sticker seals, a handwritten note, a care card.
- A free sample (mini candle or wax melt) for repeat or large orders.
- Make it photo-worthy to encourage customers to share it.
- Include social handles and the email-signup QR code.

|---|---|

| Feb | Valentine's gifts (start promoting early January) |
| Mar–Apr | Spring florals, Easter, Mother's Day pre-launch |
| May | Mother's Day, teacher appreciation, wedding season |

| Oct | Halloween, autumn bestsellers |
| **Nov–Dec** | **Biggest season.** Gift sets, Black Friday/Cyber Monday, shipping cutoff reminders. Stock up by September. |

Start promoting each season **6–8 weeks ahead**; Etsy shoppers search early.

## 11. Partnerships & Outreach

- **Micro-influencers** (2k–20k followers) in home, lifestyle, or wellness: offer a free candle for an honest post.
- **Local:** farmers markets, craft fairs, boutique wholesale or consignment (also a good way to get photos and testimonials).
- **Collaborations:** pair with a tea maker, soap maker, or bookshop for cross-promotion.
- **Gift guides & press:** pitch bloggers and local media for holiday gift roundups.
- **Corporate/wedding favors:** add a listing for bulk and custom-label orders.

## 12. Safety & Trust

Customers (and Etsy) expect: warning labels, burn-time guidance, wick-trimming instructions, and compliant labeling for your region (e.g., CPSC guidelines in the US). Mention these on your listings and in your care card.

## 15. Metrics to Track (Monthly)

- Etsy: visits, conversion rate, favorites, revenue, top listings, search terms
- Ads: spend, revenue, ROAS (aim for at least 2–3×)
- Social: reach, engagement, link clicks
- Email: subscribers, open rate, click rate
- Business: average order value, repeat-customer rate, profit margin

**Rule of thumb:** if a listing gets views but no sales, fix photos and price. If it gets no views, fix SEO. If it sells well, promote it.
