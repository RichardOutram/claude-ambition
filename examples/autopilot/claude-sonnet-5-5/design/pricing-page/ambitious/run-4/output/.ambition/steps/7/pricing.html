Questions, answered

Are there fees beyond the plan price?

No. You pay the plan price and nothing else to us. We never take a cut of your payments. Your payment processor (for example Stripe) charges its own standard rate, and that is the only other cost.

Can I leave, and what happens to my data?

Cancel in two clicks from settings, with no contracts or cancellation fees. Paid plans have a 30-day money-back guarantee; after that we don’t refund unused months, but you keep access until the period you paid for ends. Then your account becomes read-only for 90 days, and you can download invoices, clients and payment history as CSV or PDF at any time, on any plan.

How is my data protected?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest and backed up daily. We never sell it or share it with advertisers.

Will my clients see Ledgerly branding?

On Starter, invoices carry a small “Sent with Ledgerly” footer. Paid plans remove it, and your logo and colors are on every invoice on every plan. Studio can also host the client portal on your own domain.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Starter is free forever, and the 14-day trials of Freelancer and Studio don't ask for one either.

What happens if I go over the Starter limits?

Nothing breaks. Existing invoices stay accessible, and we'll offer an upgrade when you want to add more clients or invoices.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?

Yes, 50% off for students and registered nonprofits. Contact support with proof of status and we'll sort it out.
