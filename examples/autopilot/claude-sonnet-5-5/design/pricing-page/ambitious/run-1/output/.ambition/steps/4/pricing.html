Questions, answered

Am I locked in?

No. There's no contract. Cancel monthly plans anytime, and yearly plans get a 30-day refund. After cancelling you drop to Starter, and your invoices, clients and history stay accessible.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, on every plan including Starter. Export clients, invoices, payments and expenses as CSV, and every invoice as PDF, whenever you like and also after you cancel.

Do you charge fees on my payments?

Never. Card payments cost 2.9% + 30¢ and bank transfers 0.8% (max $5), all of which goes to the payment processor. You can also pass the fee on to your client.

What happens if I go over 5 or 25 clients?

Nothing is deleted or locked. You can keep working with your existing clients, and we'll ask you to upgrade before you add another.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Starter is free forever and both trials start without a card.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.
