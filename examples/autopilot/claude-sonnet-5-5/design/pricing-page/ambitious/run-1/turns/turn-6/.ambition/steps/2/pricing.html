Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Starter is free forever and the paid trials need no card. You'll only be asked for one if you decide to keep the plan.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately and we prorate the difference. If you downgrade, your data stays safe; you just lose access to the higher-tier features.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

We never add fees of our own. Standard card processor fees (2.9% + 30¢) go to the payment provider, and bank transfers are free.

What happens if I go over the Starter limits?

We'll let you know, and you can finish what you're working on. Nothing is deleted or locked; you'd just need to upgrade to create more.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?

Yes, 30% off any paid plan. Get in touch with us and we'll set it up.
