Questions, answered

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your settings whenever you like. Changes are prorated and there are no cancellation fees.

What happens if I go over the free plan limits?

We’ll let you know and never delete anything. You can still view and download all your data; you’ll just need to upgrade to create more invoices that month.

Do you charge extra for getting paid online?

No. Inkwell adds no markup. You only pay the standard processing fee from our payment partner, and it’s the same on every plan.

Is there a discount for students or nonprofits?

Yes — contact support for 30% off any paid plan if you qualify.

Can I export my data?

Always. Export invoices, clients, and reports as CSV or PDF on any plan, including after you cancel.
