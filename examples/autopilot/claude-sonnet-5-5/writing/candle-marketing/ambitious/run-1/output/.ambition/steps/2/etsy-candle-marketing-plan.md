## 2. Brand Positioning

- **Who you are:** [e.g., small-batch soy candles hand-poured in (city)]
- **Unique angle (pick one and own it):** scent storytelling, eco-friendly materials, a signature aesthetic, gift-ready packaging, or a themed collection (e.g., "cozy literary candles")
- **Brand voice:** warm, sensory, personal, with a maker's touch
- **Visual identity:** consistent palette, label design, and photography style across Etsy, social, and packaging

## 3. Target Customers

1. **Gift buyers** (birthdays, housewarming, teachers, bridesmaids, corporate gifts). They are high volume and care about packaging and shipping speed.
2. **Self-care and home-decor shoppers.** They care about aesthetics, scent, and clean ingredients.
3. **Seasonal shoppers** (fall, winter holidays, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day).
4. **Event buyers** (weddings, bridal showers, party favors). They place bulk orders.

## 4. Etsy SEO (Highest-Leverage Channel)

### Keyword research
- Use Etsy search autocomplete, eRank, Marmalead, or EverBee.
- Target long-tail phrases: "soy candle gift for her," "lavender candle hand poured," "bridesmaid candle favor."

### Listing optimization checklist
- [ ] **Title:** put the primary keyword first and use all 140 characters naturally
- [ ] **13 tags**, all used, mixing broad and long-tail phrases
- [ ] **Attributes** filled in (scent, occasion, material, color)
- [ ] **Description:** first 160 characters carry the keywords and the hook, followed by burn time, wax type, wick, size, and care instructions
- [ ] **10 photos + a video** per listing (see Section 6)
- [ ] **Categories** set precisely
- [ ] Review and refresh the lowest performers monthly

### Ranking factors to manage
- Fast processing and shipping times
- Conversion rate (strong photos and pricing)
- Review quantity and quality
- Fresh activity (new listings and renewals)
- Free shipping threshold or free shipping built into price

## 5. Product Strategy

- **Hero product:** one best-selling scent/size to feature everywhere
- **Collections:** seasonal (Autumn, Winter, Spring, Summer) plus year-round staples
- **Price ladder:**
  - Entry: mini candles / wax melts / sample sets ($8–14)
  - Core: 8 oz jar ($22–28)
  - Premium: large or multi-wick, gift sets ($38–65)
- **Gift sets and bundles:** raise average order value (AOV) and appeal to gift buyers
- **Personalization:** custom labels, names, and messages for weddings and corporate orders
- **Seasonal calendar** (launch 6–8 weeks ahead):

| Event | Launch by |
|-------|-----------|
| Valentine's Day | mid-December |
| Mother's Day | early March |
| Wedding season | February |
| Back-to-school / teacher gifts | July |
| Fall scents | August |
| Black Friday / holidays | September–October |

## 6. Photography and Video

- **Main image:** clean, bright, candle clearly visible, with scale cues
- **Lifestyle shots:** lit candle in a styled room, in hands, on a shelf
- **Details:** label, wick, wax texture, packaging
- **Scale and info graphic:** size, burn time, ingredients
- **Gifting shot:** boxed with ribbon or note card
- **Video (5–15 sec):** pouring, lighting, or an unboxing clip
- Use natural light and a consistent background. Test thumbnails at small size.

## 7. Pricing, Promotions, and Etsy Tools

- Price for profit: **(materials + labor + packaging + fees + shipping) × margin**. Etsy fees include listing, transaction, payment processing, and offsite ads.
- **Sales and coupons:** use Etsy's tools for shop-wide sales (e.g., 10% off over $40) and targeted offers to abandoned-cart shoppers.
- **Free shipping guarantee** (order thresholds) can improve search placement.
- **Etsy Ads:** start at $1–3/day on your top 2–3 listings, then review after 2–4 weeks. Keep ads on listings with strong conversion and pause the rest.
- **Offsite Ads** are mandatory above certain sales levels, so factor the fee into pricing.

## 8. Social Media Strategy

### Channels (focus on 2, not 5)
| Platform | Why | Content |
|----------|-----|---------|
| **Instagram** | Visual, gift-driven audience | Reels, carousels, Stories |
| **TikTok** | Reach through "behind the scenes" and ASMR content | Pouring, packing orders, scent reviews |
| **Pinterest** | Evergreen traffic, great for home decor and gifts | Pins linking to listings |

### Content pillars
1. **Process:** pouring, wicking, curing, labeling, packing orders
2. **Product:** scent notes, new drops, restocks
3. **Lifestyle:** cozy scenes, gift ideas, seasonal moods
4. **Social proof:** reviews, customer photos, unboxings
5. **Story:** why you started, your studio, small-business life

### Cadence
- 3–4 posts/week plus daily Stories
- 1 batch-filming session per week
- Always include a link to your Etsy shop (link in bio, Pinterest pins, UTM links)

## 9. Email and Customer Retention

- **Collect emails:** include a card in every package ("10% off your next order"), a signup link in your Etsy shop announcement, and social bio links
- **Platform:** Mailchimp, Klaviyo, or MailerLite free tier
- **Flows:**
  - Welcome series (3 emails: story, bestsellers, discount)
  - Post-purchase thank-you with burn and care tips
  - Review request after delivery
  - Restock/replenish reminder at 6–8 weeks
  - Seasonal launch announcements
- **Packaging inserts:** handwritten note, care card, QR code to review or follow you
- **Loyalty:** return-customer coupon codes and early access to new scents

## 10. Reviews and Social Proof

- Ask politely in a thank-you note and a follow-up Etsy message
- Respond to every review, positive or negative
- Feature good reviews in listing photos and social posts
- Resolve issues quickly. Good service often turns a complaint into a 5-star review.

## 11. Partnerships and Outreach

- **Micro-influencers** (2k–20k followers) in home, wellness, and gifting niches: send free product in exchange for honest content
- **Local retail:** pitch boutiques, coffee shops, and salons for wholesale or consignment
- **Markets and pop-ups:** also a good source of email signups
- **Collaborations:** pair with other makers (tea, soap, ceramics) for gift bundles
- **Bulk/corporate gifting:** build a dedicated listing for 10+ orders
- **Gift guides and press:** pitch local blogs and seasonal roundups

| Metric | Where | Frequency |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Visits, views, conversion rate | Etsy Stats | Weekly |
| Top search keywords | Etsy Stats | Monthly |
| Ad ROAS (revenue ÷ ad spend) | Etsy Ads dashboard | Weekly |
| Average order value | Etsy dashboard | Monthly |

| Repeat purchase rate | Etsy / manual | Quarterly |
| Net profit margin | Spreadsheet | Monthly |

**Rule of thumb:** if views are low, work on SEO and traffic. If views are high but sales are low, work on photos, price, and listing copy.

## 15. Risks and Fixes

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Seasonal sales swings | Diversify scents/products and build email list |
| Shipping damage (glass jars) | Test packaging, use inserts, and offer replacements |
| Heavy competition | Differentiate with niche themes and personalization |
| Rising fees and ad costs | Review pricing quarterly and grow direct traffic (email, social) |
| Burnout | Batch tasks, automate emails, and set a weekly schedule |
| Safety / compliance | Include warning labels, follow candle labeling rules in your region, and consider product liability insurance |

## 16. Weekly Routine

- **Monday:** review stats, respond to messages and reviews
- **Tuesday:** pour and make candles, film process content
- **Wednesday:** post content, list or refresh 1–2 listings
- **Thursday:** outreach (influencers, shops, collabs)
- **Friday:** send email or promotion, plan next week's content
- **Weekend:** pack and ship, engage on social
