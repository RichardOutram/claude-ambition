Follow the ambition method. The plugin adds it to the conversation the first time you run a step.

Before doing anything, ask me up to 4 questions, one at a time: what this is ultimately for; what would make the result unmistakably excellent (specific and hard, not "good"); what would prove it that isn't you; what access or tools you need that you lack. Then restate the task at the level that serves the goal, say what you changed, and proceed. Raise every part of the original ask too, as the same kind of thing: altitude adds the goal beyond it on top, never replaces it or leaves any part of it as it was. If the work feeds decisions, aim it at ending in the most urgent ones, each with possible directions and the arguments for and against. Name its genre bar: what its users and best practitioners judge it on, including what can only be judged by looking at it, reading it or using it.
