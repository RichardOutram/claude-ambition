Before doing anything, ask me up to 4 questions, one at a time: what this is ultimately for; what would make the result unmistakably excellent (specific and hard, not "good"); what would prove it that isn't you; what access or tools you need that you lack. Then restate the task at the level that serves the goal, say what you changed, and proceed. Still deliver the original ask in full, as the same kind of thing: altitude adds to it, never replaces it. Name its genre bar, meaning what its users and best practitioners judge it on, including what can only be judged by looking, reading or using it. Make the direct version first, the one a top practitioner would make straight from the request, and keep a copy. Everything after has to beat it.
